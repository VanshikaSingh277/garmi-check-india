Garmi Check India: how the whole app works

A mobile-first website that gives anyone in India their heat level right now, an hour-by-hour plan for their home and only evidence-backed fixes, cheapest first. It does the same for colonies and governments, with a district ranking. ML predicts temperatures; published rules decide safety; every number has a source.

4Flow A: finding your place

Three ways in, one offline lookup, one place card.

📍 Use my locationBrowser watchPosition for ≤ 15 s; keeps the best fix and stops at ≤ 50 m. Shows "Improving accuracy… ±900 m → ±30 m".Denied / timeout / no signal / not https → a clear message + search instead. Accuracy > 1 km → "approximate, drag the pin".
🔎 Search/api/search?q= takes a 6-digit PIN (or its first digits), a town, locality or village. Old names and Hindi names work (Bombay → Mumbai, कोलकाता).PIN results are labelled "approximate area".
🗺️ Tap or drag the pinAny point on the India map. India-wide zoom shows only official district outlines; street tiles appear from zoom 7.

districts.district_atPoint-in-polygon on the LGD districts → the official district, plus its summer heat profile and index rank.

6The decision logic

Four pieces: the heat level (rules), the room temperature (ML), the 24-hour plan (ML output + verified limits) and the choice of fixes (filter).

6.2 Room temperature: the ML part

XGBoost regressorTrained on 94,955 hourly readings from 56 measured Delhi homes (Apr–Jun 2016, open CC0 dataset). 21 inputs:
  • outdoor temperature, humidity, sun and wind
  • lags of 1, 2, 3, 6, 12 and 24 h, plus the 24 h mean and max
  • 6 h sun total and hour of day
  • fan and cooler
  • roof type (4 categories)
±1.52 °Caverage error on homes the model never saw (leave-home-out CV)
±2.11 °Ca simple rule baseline, for comparison
OutputRoom temperature for each of the next 24 hours, shown as an 80 % range (pred − 2.21 to pred + 2.68 °C).
GuardHidden if fewer than 80 % of the next 24 h are inside the training range (outdoor 22.2–42.1 °C, RH 9–89 %). Outside Delhi it is labelled "not yet tested here".

6.3 The 24-hour plan (uses the ML output, adds no new thresholds)

🪟 Windows, per hour
OPEN when the forecast outside air is below the low end of the room's predicted range
SHUT + curtains when it is above the high end
either in between
No room estimate → no window advice.
🌀 Fan, per hourThe verified fan limits applied to the room estimate (or to outdoor air when there is no estimate):
helps below 35 °C
not enough alone from 35 °C
don't rely on it from 38 °C (older/vulnerable) or 39 °C (adults)
🔥 Hottest hoursThe 3 warmest hours of the room estimate (or of the outdoor heat index) are outlined in the hour grid.Example (Jaipur, 15 Oct): open windows 16:00–10:00, room hottest 13:00–15:00.

How the district index is made

Exposure (E)ERA5 via Open-Meteo, 1 Apr–30 Jun 2021–2025, at each district's population-weighted centre: mean day max, mean night min, mean max wet-bulb, and days ≥ 40 °C.
Sensitivity (S)WorldPop 2025 at 1 km, summed per district: share aged 65+, share under 5, and population density.
IndexEach measure is percentile-ranked across India; E and S are averaged, then ranked 1 to 734. Equal weights agree with PCA weights (Spearman 0.92).

14Honest limits

  • Room model: trained in Delhi. Elsewhere it is a rough guide until it is validated with measured homes.
  • District values: one weather point per district, placed where people live. Big districts are not uniform, and mountains read colder.
  • District index: no all-India data on household coping capacity yet, so it covers heat + sensitive people only.
  • Boundaries: 2021 LGD districts and pre-2022 Delhi wards. Newer units are not split out.
  • PIN codes: approximate. A GPS point gets no PIN.
  • Map tiles: OSM tiles appear only at regional zoom. Production should use an India-compliant tile provider.
  • Forecast: Open-Meteo's free tier is for non-commercial use, which suits the hackathon. A paid plan or IMD feeds are needed for production.
