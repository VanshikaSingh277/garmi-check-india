# Phase 8: Latest Research Review (2024–2026) and Changes Adopted

Run: 15 Oct 2026.

## What's new, and whether it changes the site

| Topic | New evidence | Effect on the site |
|---|---|---|
| **Fan safety** | Meade 2024 *Lancet Planet Health* review (advise against fans >35 °C, especially older adults). O'Connor 2025 RCT (36 °C/45% RH, 8 h: no core-temperature benefit in older adults). Chaseling 2025 *JAMA Netw Open* (fan **worsens** core temperature at 45 °C/15% RH; skin wetting helps). O'Connor 2026 *J Am Geriatr Soc* (ceiling fan at 31 °C: −0.2 °C, "not wholly efficacious"). | **Already adopted in Phase 1.** Stricter-wins rule (35/38 °C for vulnerable people; 35/39 °C for healthy adults); skin-wetting advice is qualitative only. |
| **Indoor heat in Indian homes** | Climate Trends (Oct 2025–Apr 2026), 50 Chennai homes: indoors rarely below 31 °C, often above 34 °C hours after sunset, RH above 75% *(news coverage only, primary report not read, so UNVERIFIED)*. | **Consistent with our Delhi data:** rooms stay 2–4.5 °C warmer than outside at night. Supports the night-airing advice and the night indoor range. No change. |
| **Cool roofs** | REFLECT trials (Ahmedabad, Burkina Faso, Mexico, Niue, Fiji) are registered, but **no peer-reviewed results yet**. Burkina early results (1.2–1.7 °C) come from news only. A **2026 scoping review** (*Curr Environ Health Rep*, PMID 42690478) finds a lack of empirical evidence in structurally vulnerable communities. | **Wording changed:** the cool-roof effect now reads "small Indian field studies … (large trials still running)" in EN and HI. The effect size stays at the conservative 1.0–1.4 °C. |
| **Heat action plans / early warning** | Still only one measured mortality evaluation in India (Ahmedabad, Hess 2018). 2025 World Bank / *npj Urban Sustain.* benefit-cost work is modelling-based (warning systems and greening are cost-effective in scenarios). | No change. The site does not claim lives saved. |
| **ML for Indian urban heat** | Jaipur Random Forest UHI model (ISPRS Annals 2026, R² 0.806). HiTIC-India: LightGBM-downscaled daily 1 km human thermal index, 2003–2020 (ESSD preprint 2026; RMSE 3.1 °C). Resilience360 AI tool for the Delhi HAP (ICRS 2026). | Our Model A (spatial-CV R² 0.82) is in line. **Future:** evaluate HiTIC-India as a 1 km heat-index layer once peer-reviewed. Resilience360 is related work to cite in the pitch. |
| **Vegetation in humid heat** | arXiv 2025: transpiring vegetation can *raise* heat index in humid urban climates. | The site makes **no** numeric greening claim (effect not verified). Any future tree-planting advice must speak of surface/air temperature, not heat index, in the humid season. |
| **Policy** | Heatwaves added to notified natural calamities (MHA, Aug 2026), so they are SDRF/NDRF eligible *(reported by Down To Earth and Mongabay)*. NCDC 2026 heat advisory. | Linked in the Government mode "Schemes & guidance". |

## Nothing found that contradicts a number the site uses.
